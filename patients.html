Why Patients Love SOMAVAC

Designed Around Your Comfort

For 50 years, patients have dealt with awkward, leaky JP drain bulbs after surgery. SOMAVAC® is the first real upgrade, a smart pump that makes recovery easier, quieter, and less stressful.

Comfortable & Discreet

Weighs just ounces and fits in a small pouch you wear at your waist, hidden under clothes. No bulky bulbs pinned inside your surgical bra or dangling at your side.

Incredibly Easy to Use

In clinical studies, patients rated SOMAVAC easier to use than a thermometer (Kortum & Peres, 2015). Simple indicator lights tell you everything you need to know, no guessing.

Whisper-Quiet

Unlike loud hospital-grade suction, SOMAVAC® operates so quietly you’ll forget it’s there. Sleep, rest, and recover without constant noise or disruption.

Drains Out Sooner

Continuous smart suction removes fluid more efficiently than traditional bulbs. Clinical data shows drains can be removed up to 30% sooner,* meaning less time tethered to tubes.

Reduced Risk of Complications

The fully closed system reduces your risk of complications. In a clinical study, SOMAVAC patients had a 0% seroma rate (vs. 13.6%). Effective drainage lowers the chance of fluid buildup, which affects 1 in 5 patients with traditional drains.

24/7 Patient Support

Have a question at 2 AM? SOMAVAC® provides a dedicated U.S.-based support line you can call anytime. You’re never alone during your recovery.

How It Works

Simple, Smart, Continuous

No stripping. No squeezing. No guessing. SOMAVAC does the work for you.

1

Your Surgeon Connects It

After surgery, your surgeon connects SOMAVAC® directly to your surgical drain tubes. It’s ready to go before you even leave the hospital.

2

Smart Suction Does the Work

Built-in sensors maintain continuous, gentle −100mmHg suction. No manual squeezing. Simple LED lights tell you the status at a glance.

3

Empty the Bag & Relax

Fluid collects in a replaceable bag. When it’s full, a light alerts you. Swap the bag in seconds, no mess, no fuss. That’s it.

The SOMAVAC® Sustained Vacuum System (SVS) is intended for the removal of bodily fluids following large flap forming plastic and general surgeries. There are potential risks associated with the use of a SOMAVAC SVS including a temporary lack of suction which could result in minor pain or engorgement because of some fluid retention. Talk to your surgeon about indications, warnings, precautions, adverse events and other important medical information. This information is for educational purposes only. Only your health care professional can determine if a SOMAVAC SVS is right for you. Patient results may vary.
