Practice Benefits

Elevate Your Standard of Care

800,000+ U.S. surgical patients require drain suction annually. 20% experience complicated seromas, costing $1B+ in interventions. SOMAVAC® addresses this with the first meaningful innovation in surgical drainage in 50 years.

Faster Drain Removal

Continuous −100mmHg suction removes fluid more expeditiously than passive bulbs. Clinical data shows drains can be removed as early as 8.3 days in obese mastectomy patients — up to 30% sooner than standard of care.

Reduced Complication Rates

The fully closed system with one-way drain connectors minimizes surgical site exposure to infectious materials. Timely drain removal reduces infection risk — which triples after 3 weeks (Hannah et al., 2016).

Fewer Drains Per Patient

Due to constant, effective fluid removal, surgeons in clinical studies reported using fewer drains per patient with SOMAVAC® — simplifying the surgical plan while maintaining effective drainage.

Reduced Reoperations

Literature confirms 1 in 5 women require reoperation with traditional drains. By removing fluid more efficiently and reducing seroma formation, SOMAVAC® helps lower reoperation rates and associated costs.

Higher Patient Satisfaction

Patients in the SOMAVAC® clinical study reported it was easier to use than an inhaler or thermometer. Patients consistently describe the experience as transformative compared to JP bulbs.

Fewer Follow-Up Visits

Faster drain removal and fewer complications mean reduced post-operative office visits — freeing clinical time, improving throughput, and reducing the administrative burden on your practice.
