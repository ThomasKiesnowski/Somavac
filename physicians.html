The Clinical Differentiator

Surface Healing vs. Deep Tissue Therapy

Surface NPWT devices like Prevena address incision healing externally. SOMAVAC® is the only device — protected by 5 US patents — that delivers continuous negative pressure directly into the deep wound space.

Seroma Prevention

Continuous −100mmHg suction removes stagnant serous fluid from the deep wound space before it can accumulate. Stagnant fluid is the precursor to seroma formation, infection, and costly reoperations.

Tissue Apposition

Deep negative pressure forces tissue layers together, eliminating dead space and enabling proper healing. Without apposition, the body fills open space with fluid — preventing tissue-to-tissue integration.

ADM Integration

In procedures using acellular dermal matrix, deep tissue therapy ensures the ADM heals to surrounding tissue rather than floating in fluid. Without integration, a $30,000 tissue investment may never incorporate.
