The Clinical Differentiator

Surface Healing vs. Deep Tissue Therapy

Surface NPWT devices address incision healing externally. SOMAVAC® is the only device that delivers continuous negative pressure directly into the deep wound space.

Seroma Prevention

Continuous −100mmHg suction removes stagnant serous fluid from the deep wound space before it can accumulate. Stagnant fluid is the precursor to seroma formation, infection, and costly reoperations.

Tissue Apposition

Deep negative pressure forces tissue layers together, eliminating dead space and enabling proper healing. Without apposition, the body fills open space with fluid — preventing tissue-to-tissue integration.

ADM Integration

In procedures using acellular dermal matrix, deep tissue therapy ensures the ADM heals to surrounding tissue rather than floating in fluid. Without integration, a $30,000 tissue investment may never incorporate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clinician FAQ

SOMAVAC drain connectors attach to 15Fr–19Fr non-collapsible drains of your choice. This gives you flexibility to use your preferred drain while benefiting from continuous deep tissue negative pressure therapy.

The SOMAVAC drain connectors are supplied in sterile packaging. The device itself is not sterile and should be attached to the drain connectors after the sterile field has been taken down.

SOMAVAC applies −100mmHg at the end of the drains, controlled by a built-in pressure sensor. The pumps continuously seek −100mmHg to generate consistent, continuous suction around the clock.

SOMAVAC accepts 1–4 drains. There are two pumps per device. If more than 2 drains are used, drains 1A & 1B are recorded by pump 1, and drains 2A & 2B are recorded by pump 2. Each side of the fluid collection bag holds 50cc (100cc total).

Yes. SOMAVAC has been cleared by the FDA for use in post-operative drainage management since 2018. It is indicated for the removal of bodily fluids following large flap forming plastic and general surgeries.

SOMAVAC is covered within the overall cost of surgery. It is available in select hospitals. Please reach out to us if you would like to use SOMAVAC with your patients.

The SOMAVAC device must be kept dry. If allowed by the physician, negative pressure therapy may be disconnected temporarily to allow showering. Instructions are included in the patient kit.
