The SOMAVAC Answer

The First & Only Deep Tissue Negative Pressure Therapy

Negative pressure wound therapy is proven and standard of care — but until now, it only worked at the surface. SOMAVAC® SVS uses continuous sensor-controlled suction to deliver −100mmHg negative pressure directly into the deep wound space, where it matters most.

This deep tissue therapy removes stagnant fluid, creates tissue apposition so layers heal together properly, and enables integration of acellular dermal matrix (ADM) tissue. The result: drains out up to 30% sooner, fewer seromas, and reduced complication rates.

Worn discreetly on a belt under clothing and as flat as an iPhone, the fully closed system accepts 1–4 surgical drains through one-way connectors — eliminating manual bulb compression and minimizing infection risk.

How It Works

Smart Suction, Simplified

01

Connect

The surgeon connects SOMAVAC® SVS to 1–4 surgical drains via one-way connectors, creating a clean, fully closed system that minimizes exposure to infectious materials.

02

Wear & Go

As flat as an iPhone, the pump attaches to a belt worn discreetly under clothing. Fluid collects in a marked bag for easy measurement and disposal. No safety pins. No “grenade” bulbs.

03

Recover Faster

Continuous sensor-controlled suction maintains −100mmHg around the clock, delivering Deep Tissue Negative Pressure Therapy directly into the wound space. Fluid removed faster. Tissue layers heal together. Drains out up to 30% sooner.
